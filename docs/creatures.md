# Creature behavior and interactions

- Other animals live in `src/creatures/`, with navigation and effects in supporting modules. The giant tortoise (`assets-src/tortoise.py`, faces -Y in Blender like the lizard) walks the oval `TORTOISE_ROUTE` (`src/world/layout.ts`) on a kinematic body, fitted to the ground under its feet. Its convex shell collider is built from carapace points the script exports (`shell_hull` extra) and rises and falls with the drawn shell's body bone, so the lizard can ride it; a lizard standing on it is carried along (`PlayerController.carry`). It tramples plants under it, sometimes stops to eat the one at its mouth, rests now and then, and shoves the lizard aside with `PlayerController.shove`. Walking onto a lizard in its path ahead of its middle tramples it: half its health (`VITALS.trampleHit`) and a 0.25 s throw out to the side, clear of its path (`Wounds.trample`, `TRAMPLE_CLEAR`), then not again for 3 s; hook `tortoise().tramples`. `Regrowth` (`src/creatures/route.ts`) keeps a meadow along the route and sprouts seedlings on it.

- Other marine iguanas (`src/creatures/iguana.ts`): three on the lava shore, each the player's own lizard (a `PlayerController`, `MovementStateMachine`, `LizardModel` and `LizardVisual`) driven by its own `InputState` instead of the keyboard, tinted per iguana. They bask on black lava only (`lavaCover` ≥ 0.85), broadside to the sun, or alongside a lizard (the player or another iguana) already lying still on lava nearby, walking in along its line. They sneeze salt (`saltSpray.ts`, head nod via `LizardModel.nod`), and when hungry walk into the sea, swim and dive to a grown algae patch, nose in until the drawn snout is in its fronds, bite it down and eat it, maybe go on to another, and come back out to bask; they keep off the rock piles. A body in their way they walk round by a point beside it, keeping to the side they picked, and they come in to lie beside a mate along a line off its end, lying down once roughly level with it and near the line (`MATE_LEVEL`, `MATE_LINE`) rather than pushing on for the exact spot; they give way rather than push. They climb onto rocks and logs but never a cactus: their `climbGroups` leave out `CACTUS_GROUP`, their stuck-hop skips a cactus in the way, and one that lands on a top anyway hops straight off (`Spines.shake`, no harm done). The lizard walking into one pushes it slowly aside (`Iguanas.pushedBy`, from `PlayerController.blockers`), and the camera's arm passes through them. Bodies are solid, in `IGUANA_GROUP`; crabs' rays and the `groundAt` hook look past them (`IGNORE_STEMS_AND_IGUANAS`). A still lizard is stepped with `PlayerController.stillGroups` so Rapier's character controller can't let it creep beside a moving body. A hawk's shadow over one sends it bolting for cover or freezing; two strikes put it down (`IGUANA_LIVES`) and once eaten it comes back 45 to 80 s later. Hooks `iguanas()`, `iguanaDo(i, 'feed' | 'move' | 'sneeze')`, `iguanaPlace(i, x, z, yaw)`, `iguanaScare(i, x, z)`, `saltSpray()`.

- Sally Lightfoot crabs (`src/creatures/crab.ts`, model `assets-src/crab.py`, faces -Y in Blender, +X its left) live on the lava shore and the rock piles. They're drawn, not simulated: no collider, kept on the surface by downward rays that ignore the lizard. Each walks sideways round its home, grazes (at algae above the water when there's some), hops any step from 5 mm up to 8 cm (the hop clips stay in place; the game carries it through the air), runs from the lizard and ducks if cornered, and never goes below `WATER_Y`. A crab under the lizard's body is pushed out from under it, and crabs keep a leg span apart. There are 18 of them. Once the lizard has lain still for 4 s crabs stop fearing it, and the nearest one with a clear way walks over, hops onto its back (`LizardModel.back`) and grooms it with the graze clip until the lizard moves; any still iguana is a `Host` they groom the same way (hook `crabs()` says whom with `grooming`). Crabs fear any iguana that isn't calm (the player or another, up and about or not yet still for 4 s; the others start calm), and a crab that gets no nearer where it's going for a second gives up. A crab the hawk catches dies where it stands, is eaten away and walks back out onto the rocks 30 to 60 s later. Rays onto a rock's convex hull are a few mm noisy, so tests check crab heights to within a few mm.

- The Galapagos hawk (`src/creatures/hawk.ts`, model `assets-src/hawk.py`, faces -Y in Blender) hunts the player, the other iguanas and the crabs. It's drawn, not simulated: a kinematic flyer whose heading turns at a limited rate, banking and pitching with its velocity, on Hermite curves for take-off, landing and the strike. It perches on the tallest open rocks (the rock piles' crests among them) and on low trees and cactus by the coast (`src/creatures/perches.ts`: spots found with rays onto the drawn meshes where its toes rest on the level and its tail, angled down and back, clears what's behind, from the `perch_tail` extra), and rests there: it doesn't hunt from a perch, and the lizard coming within 0.5 m flushes it to the perch furthest from the lizard (`FLUSH`). On the wing it soars a patrol circle, and once it has seen the lizard for a second (`src/world/cover.ts`: hidden under water, in a standing plant's crown, or behind anything solid, tree crowns and cactus included; it watches five spheres from head to tail base and only sees an animal with more than half of them in view, `SEEN_SHARE`, so a head poking out of cover isn't enough) it stalks overhead, stoops and strikes with its talons. Losing sight in the stoop it pulls up, and a strike on something gone mostly into cover misses. A strike that lands (`src/player/wounds.ts`) throws the lizard 8 cm sideways with a `flinch_left`/`flinch_right` clip layered over the gait (root, spine and tail: it tips away from the blow), a puff of dust and feathers (`strikePuff.ts`), a camera shake (`FollowCamera.shake`) and a red flash at the screen's edges, and takes 40% of its health (`VITALS.hawkHit`); from full, the third knocks it down (`collapse` clip, input ignored: it buckles, rolls onto its right side and its legs settle into rest poses searched for in the script, clear of the ground) and lies there 5 s, a "Respawn in" countdown showing from the moment it falls, then comes back at the spawn. Out of sight for 3 s the hawk gives up; health then mends only slowly, through the vitals (docs/player.md). A lizard riding the tortoise is out of its reach (`Quarry.safe`, `Tortoise.ridden`): it won't go for it, and pulls out of a hunt, even mid-dive, the moment the lizard gets on. It stays calm for the first 25 s. What it can hunt it sees through `src/creatures/quarry.ts`: a `Quarry` per lizard, iguana and crab, with an `acuity` (how far off it picks that kind out, and how long it takes to be sure) and an `appeal` (the chance it bothers once it has one in its eye, a crab being barely worth the dive). One strike kills a crab, two put an iguana down, three the player. Having killed, the hawk lands on the body and plays the `feed` clip for a while: the crab or iguana then disappears and comes back later somewhere else (`Crab.kill`/`eaten`/`revive`, `Iguana.strike`/`eaten`/`comeBack`), and over the player it stands eating until the respawn. Its shadow (`castShadow`, projected along `SUN_OFFSET` while it is low) sends crabs running and iguanas bolting or freezing (`Quarry.scare`, `Iguana.scare`, `Crab.scare`). Clips glide, flap, stoop, strike, perch, land, take_off, feed; contact times are glTF extras. Hooks `hawk()`, `hawkClips()`, `hawkDo('hunt' | 'hunt_iguana' | 'hunt_crab' | 'off' | 'on' | 'perch' | 'soar')`, `hawkPerches()`, `hawkSit(i)`, `hawkLowest()`, `inSight()`, `lizardInView()`, `wounds()`, `shelters()`; other specs call `hawkDo('off')` at boot.

Iguana steering, obstacle avoidance and recovery live in `src/creatures/iguanaNavigation.ts`; activity, feeding and social spot selection stay in `iguana.ts`. The navigation module owns the goal and recovery timers, sharing the existing feet and input objects with the iguana.


- Reef fish (`src/creatures/fish.ts`, models in `assets-src/reef.py`, facing -Y in Blender like the rest): four schools of black-striped salema out in open water, four of sergeant majors and three of yellowtail surgeonfish round sunken boulders (the surgeonfish low over the bottom), and three pairs of king angelfish, one school of each kind in each stretch of the coast. Drawn, not simulated: one instanced mesh per species, the body waving side to side in the vertex shader (per-instance `aSwim`: tail-beat phase and amplitude, quicker when faster). Each school wanders round its home on a slowly turning heading at its own height over the `SeaBed`, its fish keeping together and apart, and turns back from shallows, rocks breaking the surface and the world's edge; hard limits keep every fish in the water, over the bottom and inside the walls. The lizard or another iguana coming within a kind's `fear` makes each fish near it dart away at `dash` speed and stay quick for `PANIC` seconds. They dart at full `dash` speed whatever the distance, forgetting the school, so nothing swimming can run one down in open water. Hemmed in by shallows, a rock or the world's edge (`escape`: no open water `ESCAPE` lengths ahead), a fish takes the clear heading nearest straight away, sideways or back past the threat at a slant, keeps to it while it stays clear (`out`, so it doesn't dither), and sprints at `SPRINT` times its dash; up against a hard limit it slides along it. The lizard's body spheres (`lizard.bodySpheres`) touching a fish (a segment snout to tail, `FLANK` thick) count it touched (`Fishes.touched`, the "Touch a fish" goal) and send it off: that takes coming in faster than they react: dropping on one from a jump. Chasing a school into the shallows hardly ever corners one. They cost little, so tests leave them swimming. Hooks `fish()` and `fishTouched()`.
