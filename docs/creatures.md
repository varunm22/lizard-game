# Creature behavior and interactions

- Other animals live in `src/creatures/`, with navigation and effects in supporting modules. The giant tortoise (`assets-src/tortoise.py`, faces -Y in Blender like the lizard) walks the oval `TORTOISE_ROUTE` (`src/world/layout.ts`) on a kinematic body, fitted to the ground under its feet. Its convex shell collider is built from carapace points the script exports (`shell_hull` extra) and rises and falls with the drawn shell's body bone, so the lizard can ride it; a lizard standing on it is carried along (`PlayerController.carry`). It tramples plants under it, sometimes stops to eat the one at its mouth, rests now and then, and shoves the lizard aside with `PlayerController.shove`. `Regrowth` (`src/creatures/route.ts`) keeps a meadow along the route and sprouts seedlings on it.

- Other marine iguanas (`src/creatures/iguana.ts`): three on the lava shore, each the player's own lizard (a `PlayerController`, `MovementStateMachine`, `LizardModel` and `LizardVisual`) driven by its own `InputState` instead of the keyboard, tinted per iguana. They bask on black lava only (`lavaCover` ≥ 0.85), broadside to the sun, or alongside a lizard (the player or another iguana) already lying still on lava nearby, walking in along its line. They sneeze salt (`saltSpray.ts`, head nod via `LizardModel.nod`), and when hungry walk into the sea, swim and dive to a grown algae patch, nose in until the drawn snout is in its fronds, bite it down and eat it, maybe go on to another, and come back out to bask; they keep off the rock piles. A body in their way they walk round by a point beside it, keeping to the side they picked, and they come in to lie beside a mate along a line off its end, lying down once roughly level with it and near the line (`MATE_LEVEL`, `MATE_LINE`) rather than pushing on for the exact spot; they give way rather than push. The lizard walking into one pushes it slowly aside (`Iguanas.pushedBy`, from `PlayerController.blockers`), and the camera's arm passes through them. Bodies are solid, in `IGUANA_GROUP`; crabs' rays and the `groundAt` hook look past them (`IGNORE_STEMS_AND_IGUANAS`). A still lizard is stepped with `PlayerController.stillGroups` so Rapier's character controller can't let it creep beside a moving body. A hawk's shadow over one sends it bolting for cover or freezing; two strikes put it down (`IGUANA_LIVES`) and once eaten it comes back 45 to 80 s later. Hooks `iguanas()`, `iguanaDo(i, 'feed' | 'move' | 'sneeze')`, `iguanaPlace(i, x, z, yaw)`, `saltSpray()`.

- Sally Lightfoot crabs (`src/creatures/crab.ts`, model `assets-src/crab.py`, faces -Y in Blender, +X its left) live on the lava shore and the rock piles. They're drawn, not simulated: no collider, kept on the surface by downward rays that ignore the lizard. Each walks sideways round its home, grazes (at algae above the water when there's some), hops any step from 5 mm up to 8 cm (the hop clips stay in place; the game carries it through the air), runs from the lizard and ducks if cornered, and never goes below `WATER_Y`. A crab under the lizard's body is pushed out from under it, and crabs keep a leg span apart. There are 18 of them. Once the lizard has lain still for 4 s crabs stop fearing it, and the nearest one with a clear way walks over, hops onto its back (`LizardModel.back`) and grooms it with the graze clip until the lizard moves; any still iguana is a `Host` they groom the same way (hook `crabs()` says whom with `grooming`). Crabs fear any iguana that isn't calm (the player or another, up and about or not yet still for 4 s; the others start calm), and a crab that gets no nearer where it's going for a second gives up. A crab the hawk catches dies where it stands, is eaten away and walks back out onto the rocks 30 to 60 s later. Rays onto a rock's convex hull are a few mm noisy, so tests check crab heights to within a few mm.

- The Galapagos hawk (`src/creatures/hawk.ts`, model `assets-src/hawk.py`, faces -Y in Blender) hunts the player, the other iguanas and the crabs. It's drawn, not simulated: a kinematic flyer whose heading turns at a limited rate, banking and pitching with its velocity, on Hermite curves for take-off, landing and the strike. It perches on the tallest open rocks (the rock piles' crests among them) and on low trees and cactus by the coast (`src/creatures/perches.ts`: spots found with rays onto the drawn meshes where its toes rest on the level and its tail, angled down and back, clears what's behind, from the `perch_tail` extra), and rests there: it doesn't hunt from a perch, and the lizard coming within 0.5 m flushes it to the perch furthest from the lizard (`FLUSH`). On the wing it soars a patrol circle, and once it has seen the lizard for a second (`src/world/cover.ts`: hidden under water, in a standing plant's crown, or behind anything solid, tree crowns and cactus included; it watches five spheres from head to tail base and only sees an animal with more than half of them in view, `SEEN_SHARE`, so a head poking out of cover isn't enough) it stalks overhead, stoops and strikes with its talons. Losing sight in the stoop it pulls up, and a strike on something gone mostly into cover misses. A strike that lands (`src/player/wounds.ts`) throws the lizard 8 cm sideways with a `flinch_left`/`flinch_right` clip layered over the gait (root, spine and tail: it tips away from the blow), a puff of dust and feathers (`strikePuff.ts`), a camera shake (`FollowCamera.shake`) and a red flash at the screen's edges, and takes 40% of its health (`VITALS.hawkHit`); from full, the third knocks it down (`collapse` clip, input ignored: it buckles, rolls onto its right side and its legs settle into rest poses searched for in the script, clear of the ground) for a 5 s countdown, then it comes back at the spawn. Out of sight for 3 s the hawk gives up; health then mends only slowly, through the vitals (docs/player.md). A lizard riding the tortoise is out of its reach (`Quarry.safe`, `Tortoise.ridden`): it won't go for it, and pulls out of a hunt, even mid-dive, the moment the lizard gets on. It stays calm for the first 25 s. What it can hunt it sees through `src/creatures/quarry.ts`: a `Quarry` per lizard, iguana and crab, with an `acuity` (how far off it picks that kind out, and how long it takes to be sure) and an `appeal` (the chance it bothers once it has one in its eye, a crab being barely worth the dive). One strike kills a crab, two put an iguana down, three the player. Having killed, the hawk lands on the body and plays the `feed` clip for a while: the crab or iguana then disappears and comes back later somewhere else (`Crab.kill`/`eaten`/`revive`, `Iguana.strike`/`eaten`/`comeBack`), and over the player it stands eating until the respawn. Its shadow (`castShadow`, projected along `SUN_OFFSET` while it is low) sends crabs running and iguanas bolting or freezing (`Quarry.scare`, `Iguana.scare`, `Crab.scare`). Clips glide, flap, stoop, strike, perch, land, take_off, feed; contact times are glTF extras. Hooks `hawk()`, `hawkClips()`, `hawkDo('hunt' | 'hunt_iguana' | 'hunt_crab' | 'off' | 'on' | 'perch' | 'soar')`, `hawkPerches()`, `hawkSit(i)`, `hawkLowest()`, `inSight()`, `lizardInView()`, `wounds()`, `shelters()`; other specs call `hawkDo('off')` at boot.

Iguana steering, obstacle avoidance and recovery live in `src/creatures/iguanaNavigation.ts`; activity, feeding and social spot selection stay in `iguana.ts`. The navigation module owns the goal and recovery timers, sharing the existing feet and input objects with the iguana.
