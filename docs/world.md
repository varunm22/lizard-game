# Terrain, plants and water

- Plants (`src/world/plants.ts`) are drawn, not simulated, apart from a thin solid capsule at each stem (collision group `PLANT_STEM_GROUP`; ground, climb and camera queries use `IGNORE_STEMS` to skip it). They slow the lizard through `PlayerController.speedScale`, and each is an overdamped CPU spring (`plantSpring.ts`) pushed by spheres on the drawn lizard and the camera, drawn as instanced meshes bent in the vertex shader (`src/render/plantBend.ts`). Meshes come from `assets-src/plants.py`. A plant can also be trampled (laid flat, its stem off, standing back up over about a minute), eaten (removed) or sprouted as a seedling that grows in; sprouts draw from a spare instanced patch per kind.

- The world is an 8 m Galapagos-style island (`src/world/layout.ts`): a clearing at the spawn, forest to the west and north, and the coast to the east (`shore.ts`): a sandy beach in the middle, black lava at both ends, the sea at `WATER_Y` (the old pond is gone; `waterDepth`/`inOcean` say where water is). Trees, fallen logs, lava rocks and lava cactus come from `assets-src/props.py` and are scattered deterministically by `src/world/props.ts`; each is an `Obstacle` (convex-hull or cylinder collider, faded by the camera when in the way). Lava cactus trunks are in `CACTUS_GROUP`, which the other iguanas don't climb onto and whose tops prick the lizard (docs/player.md). Rocks the tests rely on (`rock-big`, `rock-mid`, `pebble`, `log`, `rock-sunk`, `rock-island`) are placed by name in `obstacles.ts`. Two rock piles (`ROCK_PILES`, built by `rockPiles.ts` from the props' flat-topped `slab_*` meshes) run from the lava out into the sea: stacked slabs, each its own obstacle, with a spine of level steps no higher than the lizard can climb, from the land or out of the water. At the land end of each pile, one shelter to a side, a broad slab lies across two low ones, leaving a gap the lizard can stand in out of sight of anything overhead (`pileShelters()`, `SHELTER`; the loose flank slabs that would fill one are left out, after drawing the same random numbers so every other slab is unchanged). Hook `shelters()`.

- Algae (`src/world/algae.ts`) are drawn, not simulated: a registry of patches by id. `bite(id, share)` shrinks a patch and the last bite removes it (`remove(id)`); every 10 to 30 s a new one sprouts on a bare site (spare sites seeded on the same rocks, or where one was eaten) and grows in over 40 s. Each instanced batch has room for all its tile's sites, so a sprout never needs a new mesh. Hooks `algae()` (with `grown`), `removeAlgae(id)`, `sproutAlgae()`.
